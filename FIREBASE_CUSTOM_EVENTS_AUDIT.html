Needs confirmationqr_collection_view qr_templates_view collection_viewThe guide gives no description and no screen could be matched without guessing. CollectionActivity is the data-collection consent step of onboarding. Needs your confirmation of which screens are meant.
Equivalent existspermission_grantedExisting CAMERA_GRANTED, READ_PHONE_STATE_GRANTED, OVERLAY_PERMISSION_GRANTED and POST_NOTIFICATIONS_GRANTED cover the same purpose, one per permission.
Equivalent existsbanner_impression native_impression interstitial_impression app_open_ad_impressionExisting ad_impression is logged on every paid ad event, with revenue. A per-format copy would duplicate it. Existing events cannot be changed, so the per-format split is the only thing lost.
Firebase-managed (unverified)banner_click native_click interstitial_clickFirebase lists ad_click as automatic when AdMob is linked to the project. The PDF does not say so and the AdMob link was not checked.
