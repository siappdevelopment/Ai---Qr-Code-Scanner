FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000)
=====================================================
QR Code & Scanner is a fast, simple and powerful QR code reader, barcode scanner and QR code generator in one app. Point your camera at any QR code or barcode and get the result in a second. Need your own code? Create a QR code or barcode in a few taps, style it and share it.

Everything works right on your phone: no sign-up and no account needed.

📷 FAST QR & BARCODE SCANNER
• Scan QR codes and barcodes instantly with your camera
• Auto-focus and pinch-style zoom for small or far-away codes
• Flashlight (torch) for scanning in the dark
• Switch between front and back camera
• Continuous (batch) scan mode: scan many codes one after another and review them together
• Vibrate and beep feedback when a code is scanned (you can turn them off)
• Open websites automatically after a scan (optional)

🖼️ SCAN FROM GALLERY
• Pick any photo or screenshot and scan the code inside it
• Crop, rotate and flip the picture to select the exact code
• Several codes in one picture? Choose the one you want to open

🛠️ CREATE QR CODES & BARCODES
Make a code for almost anything:
• Website / URL, plain text, contact, phone number, email, SMS
• Wi-Fi network (name, security type, password, hidden network)
• WhatsApp, location, calendar event, app link
• Social: Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, X (Twitter), TikTok, Snapchat, LinkedIn, Spotify, PayPal
• Barcodes: Code 128, Code 39, Code 93, Codabar, ITF, EAN-13, EAN-8, UPC-A, UPC-E, Data Matrix, PDF 417, Aztec

🎨 CUSTOMIZE YOUR QR CODE
• Pick your own colors and color palette
• Change the body pattern and the corner (eye) style
• Add a frame around the code
• Put your logo or icon in the center
• Save as image, export as PNG or SVG, and share with any app

🕘 HISTORY & FAVORITES
• Every scan and every code you create is saved on your phone
• Search your history by text, link or title
• Filter by Scanned or Created, grouped by Today, Yesterday and Earlier
• Mark favorites for quick access
• Delete one item or clear everything whenever you want

🏠 SMART HOME SCREEN LAUNCHER (OPTIONAL)
• Use the app as your home screen with a clean clock, date and weather card
• App drawer with search, long-press for App Info and Uninstall
• Quick dock for Phone, Messages, Browser and Camera
• Swipe right from the home screen to open the QR scanner
• Choose app icon size, label size and sort order (A-Z, Z-A, install date)
• Home screen widgets: scan and create shortcuts in different styles
• You decide: set it as the default home app only if you want to

✨ MORE
• Light and dark theme
• Many app languages
• Small, smooth and easy to use
• Works without sending your scans to us

HOW TO USE
1. Open the app and allow camera access
2. Point the camera at a QR code or barcode, or tap Gallery to scan a photo
3. See the result, then open, copy or share it
4. Tap Create to make your own QR code or barcode

PERMISSIONS
• Camera: needed to scan codes
• Notifications, Phone state and Display over other apps: only for optional features such as screens that appear after a call, when a charger is connected, or when an app is installed or removed. You can skip them and still use scanning and creating.
